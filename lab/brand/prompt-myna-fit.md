## How to build Mina into an Expo app

`apps/app` must run on native, and `myna-buddy.js` is a browser web component. So:

- **Web** (`Avatar.web.tsx` or a `*.web.tsx` wrapper): render `<myna-buddy size="32">` and load the script from a same-origin copy under `apps/app/public/brand/`. Copy it, don't hotlink.
- **Native:** Mina's still face (the m with eyes and the pill beak) drawn with `react-native-svg`, from the same numbers. No animation on native in this task.
- **States follow what Myna Fit already does:**
  - `mikir` while a photo is read;
  - `sukses` for records and targets;
  - a gentle `gagal` for an unclear photo or an error;
  - `idle` otherwise.

  Map them in one place.
- **Name:**
  - The mascot is **Mina** (her accessible name is "Mina"). Today's copy calls the chat persona "Myna". List every string that says "Myna" as a speaker, and propose the change in the plan.
  - "Myna" as the brand and in "Myna Fit" stays.
  - Bubble copy stays Erdi-approved and curated, never AI-generated.
