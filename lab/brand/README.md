# Myna Health icon: usage note

The Myna Health icon is concept D, "lengkung m": a monoline lowercase m with round ends and the cyan dot. It was chosen by Erdi on 2026-10-21. Every file here is generated by `node lab/build-brand.mjs`; edit that script, not the files.

The icon does not replace the wordmark. It sits next to the wordmark, or stands alone where there is no room for it (favicon, app icon, avatars). The still icon is the brand mark everywhere; the live mascot, **Mina** (`myna-buddy.js`, sheet at `lab/myna-mascot.html`), is only for "live" moments.

## Construction

- **Grid:** 64 units. Two arches of radius 9, stems ending at y = 50, stroke 8.5 with round caps and joins. The dot is r 5.5 at (54.5, 45.5).
- **Weight:** stroke ÷ arch height = 8.5 ÷ 37.5 = 0.227. That matches the wordmark's own "m": 19 px stems on an 84 px x-height = 0.226. So the icon and the wordmark read as one family.
- **Tile:** the mark is centred on its ink bounds at 62% of a navy tile. The tile radius is 22.5% (14.4 of 64).
- **Badge:** the mark is centred on its ink bounds at 60% of a full circle.
- **Lockup:** the badge is as tall as the wordmark's whole ink (rows 34–169 of `logo.png`), and the gap is 30% of the badge. Without the circle, the bare m next to "myna" read as an extra letter ("m. myna"); the circle makes it a symbol next to a name (Erdi, 2026-10-21, P-020).

## Colours

The colours are the site's tokens (P-011).

| | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#032b84` | Mark on light grounds; tile ground |
| Cyan | `#24dde2` | The dot, always, on every ground |
| White | `#ffffff` | Mark on navy or dark grounds |

The dot stays cyan on every ground. Never recolour it, and never use the mark in electric `#113bfe` (electric is for buttons).

**Known gap:** the current wordmark file is black (`#000000`). That is why the light lockup pairs a navy icon with a black wordmark. A navy, vector wordmark from the source artwork would fix it. It must not be redrawn by hand.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep at least the dot's diameter free on every side of the mark. That is 11 of 64 units, about 30% of the mark's height. In a lockup, the gap to the wordmark is fixed by the file.
- **Minimum size:**
  - bare mark: 12 px tall on screen;
  - favicon: the tile at 16 px (the bare mark is not used below 24 px);
  - lockup: 96 px wide.

## Don't

- Don't stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or gradients.
- Don't change the stroke weight or round ends, or move the dot.
- Don't put the navy mark on navy or dark photos. Use the white version, or the tile.
- Don't redraw the wordmark, or set "myna" in another typeface next to the icon.
- Don't put eyes on the still icon. The face belongs to the live mascot only.
