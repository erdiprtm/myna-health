Myna mascot: character variants

Each variant is the chosen icon (lengkung m) plus the fewest parts: eyes and one signature part. No arms, legs or clothes. The still logo has no face; these faces only exist on the live mascot. Shown large, at 96, 44 and 24 px, and in three poses on light and dark. Pick one; then the web component, the transformation and the full expression set get built.

Intip

Only pupils are added, in the two windows under the arches: the m peeks out at you. The dot stays where the logo has it and hops in sukses. Signature motion: the arches squash in a slow blink.

Jambul

White eyes on the arch tops (like Padi's eyes on the sun) and one cyan crest feather, the myna's tuft, sprouting from the first arch. Signature motion: a crest flick with a little head tilt.

Paruh

Eyes on the arch tops, and the logo's cyan dot flies up to become a small pill beak: the dot's leap is the WOW moment of the transformation. Signature motion: the beak opens to talk.

Name ideas

  1. Mina: From myna: short, warm, easy to say in Indonesian and English. Risk: it is also a common given name.
  2. Kedip: Indonesian for 'blink', after the signature motion. Playful, clearly a character, not a person's name.
  3. Jali: From jalak, the Indonesian name for the myna family. Friendly, a little cheeky, rare as a real name.
